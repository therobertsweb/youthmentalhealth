Father's Day

Today is June 15, Father’s Day. In the past Father’s Day was a very important day as we honored our fathers and all they do for us, in providing leadership for their families. However, after 65 years of feminism the father and men in general are called “toxic masculinity.” Our young women are told in our schools and colleges to hate their father and all men, since white men are accused of being the cause of all problems in our society [CRT].

The result has been that our fathers and men in general no longer play an important role in our society, while mothers and girls are taking over in many areas. Feminism, abortion, not getting married, and calling all men toxic is resulting in a lowering of population. The forces of Marxism in our society are trying to destroy the population by attacking men, fathers, and the stable family in general. No fault divorce, narcissism, and Critical Race Theory [CRT] have convinced our young women that white men are the curse upon our society. 

In 2032, our eligible voters will be 55% Millennials and Gen Zs, who are convinced that socialism is the best direction to go. Our young ladies are flocking toward socialism, while our young men are becoming more conservative. But the women who are available for marriage are radical socialistic feminists, IF they even want to get married. If we continue down this deadly path, soon we will not have a Father’s Day in America.

Many young men are not motivated to find a wife and have children. Many young men today have stopped dating and getting married. The result will be less children. That is the goal of the New World Order elite at the top of our society. Population control is what our social chaos is all about. Chaos and a bad future discourages marriages and having children. If our young women do not repent of their feminism and socialism, we can kiss America good-bye. We need strong men to defend their families and fight for the future. Right now, the women and girls are taking over society. It is time to honor our fathers on this day, or American society is going to collapse.
